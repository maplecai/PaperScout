## 1. BIG-TB: A benchmark for evaluating prediction and interpretability of sequence-based machine learning using Mycobacterium tuberculosis genomes

bioRxiv · 预印本 · 2026-11-11 · 相关性 7/10

**研究关联：方法可借鉴**

- **为什么值得看**：评估DNA基础模型在基因组序列预测和变异归因上的表现，直接涉及基因组基础模型评估和序列表示学习，与active project中的基础模型评估高度相关。
- **任务与验证范围**：输入：结核分枝杆菌基因组序列（DNA/蛋白质） → 输出：抗生素耐药性表型预测及耐药变异归因；研究物种/细胞体系：结核分枝杆菌；验证范围：跨谱系评估（摘要未说明细胞类型）
- **核心方法**：构建包含预测与归因双任务的基准测试，评估DNA和蛋白质基础模型（如Evo2）与简单CNN基线的性能。
- **主要发现与证据**：DNA基础模型在耐药性预测上未超越简单CNN基线（平均AUC 0.888 vs 0.865）；预测性能最佳的模型不一定具有最强的变异归因恢复能力。
- **可借鉴点（研究建议）**：在评估预训练基因组学模型时，可借鉴其将预测性能与特征归因解耦评估的范式，以检验模型是否学到了机制性特征。

- **阅读时核实**：论文在处理不同长度基因组序列输入时是否对基础模型进行了padding或截断，以及这些操作对预测和归因结果的具体影响？

作者：Tasmin, M., Mohanty, S., Kulkarni, S., Farhat, M. R., Green, A. G.

### 英文摘要

Foundation models aim to learn useful representations of biological sequences, but are hindered by a lack of benchmarks to jointly evaluate predictive performance and variant attribution on diverse tasks. To this end, we present the Benchmark for Interpretable prediction from Genomes of Tuberculosis, BIG-TB. We curate over 17,000 genomes with high-quality sequencing data and experimentally measured antibiotic resistance phenotypes, combined with a curated list of WHO-catalogue of resistance-associated variants, and provide these data in an ML-ready format. BIG-TB defines two tasks: (1) predictive performance of antibiotic resistance phenotypes, and (2) attribution of predictions to known resistance-conferring variants. Using our tasks, we evaluate DNA- and protein-based foundation models, showing that DNA-based foundation models do not yet outperform simple machine learning baselines (mean test AUC = 0.888 vs. 0.865 for best CNN variant vs. best Evo2 variant across drugs). In a lineage-informed evaluation across DNA and protein modalities, predictive signal for well-characterized drugs was largely preserved (mean AUC 0.846-0.963). Across both evaluation settings, models with the highest predictive performance did not necessarily achieve the strongest recovery of resistance variants, underscoring the value of evaluating both predictive accuracy and biological interpretability. Overall, BIG-TB provides a reusable resource for developing sequence representations that are both accurate and mechanistically meaningful.

## 2. Assessing the Capacity of Genomic Language Models to Generate Informative Embeddings of Viral Genome Fragments

bioRxiv · 预印本 · 2026-11-11 · 相关性 6/10

**研究关联：方法可借鉴**

- **为什么值得看**：评估基因组语言模型（DNABERT-2、Evo2等）生成嵌入的能力，与DNA语言模型和表示学习研究方向相关。
- **任务与验证范围**：输入：短病毒基因组片段 → 输出：具有分类信息的向量表示；研究物种/细胞体系：病毒（基于ICTV病毒元数据资源）；验证范围：跨病毒目（egoviruses和mriyaviruses）
- **核心方法**：基于聚类和邻域指标（ARI、precision@k等）评估多种基因组语言模型与经典k-mer/MinHash方法在生成序列嵌入上的表现。
- **主要发现与证据**：四个gLM中有三个在ARI和precision@5上优于4-mer基线，且中间层表现最佳；DNABERT-S在跨病毒目评估中表现最稳定，表明对比微调有益且大模型并非必然更优。
- **可借鉴点（研究建议）**：在评估预训练基因组学模型时，可推测中间层特征和对比微调策略可能对下游序列表征质量有显著影响，建议在我的增强子活性预测任务中关注不同层的特征提取效果。
- **迁移边界**：原论文聚焦无监督的病毒分类嵌入任务，与我的增强子活性预测（sequence-to-function）及padding影响评估存在任务差异，其关于模型层选择和微调策略的结论不能直接跨任务泛化。
- **阅读时核实**：论文在处理不同长度的病毒基因组片段输入模型时，具体采用了何种padding或截断策略，以及这是否可能影响嵌入的聚类评估结果？

### 英文摘要

Genomic language models (gLMs) trained on large volumes of nucleotide sequences are becoming an alternative to classical methods in metagenomic analysis, particularly for studying genomic "dark matter". In this work, we investigate the ability of four gLMs (DNABERT-2, DNABERT-S, ViraLM, and Evo2-7B) to generate taxonomically informative vector representations of short viral genome fragments. We constructed a balanced reference dataset of viruses based on the ICTV Virus Metadata Resource and compared the representations generated by gLMs with conventional k-mer profiles and MinHash sketches using clustering and neighborhood-based metrics (adjusted Rand index (ARI), precision@k, and CROSS precision@k). On the reference dataset, three of the four gLMs outperformed the 4-mer profile in terms of ARI and precision@5 under the same evaluation protocol. The best performance was generally achieved by intermediate model layers. Conventional k-mer profiles consistently outperformed MinHash sketches. We further evaluated the methods on two viral orders (egoviruses and mriyaviruses) absent from the reference dataset, with model layers and k-mer lengths selected exclusively on the reference dataset. DNABERT-S showed the most consistently strong performance across these two orders, ranking first on mriyaviruses and second on egoviruses, while k-mer profiles with Jensen-Shannon distance remained competitive. We also observed the consistency of representations between forward fragments and their reverse complements. Overall, the best-performing gLMs surpassed conventional baselines, although their advantage depended on the dataset and evaluation metric. The consistently strong performance of DNABERT-S suggests a potential benefit of contrastive fine-tuning and indicates that larger models are not necessarily more effective for generating taxonomically informative representations.
